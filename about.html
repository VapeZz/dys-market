Where this data comes from

Every night we save the TCGplayer market price for each Pokémon, Pokémon Japan and One Piece product, using the public daily data published by TCGCSV. Market price reflects recent completed sales on TCGplayer.

How movers are calculated

A mover's change compares today's market price with the price saved closest to 7, 30, 90 or 365 days ago. Items under $5 today, or under $1 at the start of the window, are left out so tiny prices don't produce huge percentages.

Sets

A set's 7-day change is the change in the combined market price of its sealed products. "Booster box" shows the main booster box when the set has one.

Good to know

  • Prices can lag by about a day and swing on thin sales, especially right after release.
  • These are market reference prices, not Drive Your Style's prices. Items marked "In stock" link to our store.
