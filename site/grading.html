Grading Insights

Find the cards worth sending to PSA, CGC, BGS or TAG. Pick a grading tier, where you'll sell the slab and your odds of a 10, and every card shows its expected profit after grading and selling fees.

How the estimate works

Only cards with a steady price and live listings near the market price are shown, so thin or spiking prices don't mislead the math. Promos, event and prize cards are left out. All-in cost = the lowest raw listing today + the grading fee + shipping and insurance both ways.

Grading fees come from each company's current price list, open tiers only. PSA and CGC charge by the card's graded value: when a grade is worth more than your tier covers, it's upcharged to the tier that covers it, and the cost includes that risk at your odds. Sale prices are after the selling fees of the marketplace you pick.

Expected profit = (chance of 10 × 10 price) + (chance of 9 × 9 price) + (rest × about 0.7× raw), each after selling fees, − all-in cost. Break-even 10 is the 10 price where that profit is zero at your odds; the lower it is compared to raw (shown as ×), the easier grading pays off.

Graded prices are recent eBay and Fanatics sold prices for each grade (PokemonPriceTracker), currently for English and Japanese Pokémon cards. A 10 that sells less than about once a week is marked Slow seller and hidden by default: a high price that rarely sells is hard to cash in. Odds are your estimate, not a prediction; check centering and surfaces first. Fees change often, so confirm with the grader before you submit. Taxes aren't included.
