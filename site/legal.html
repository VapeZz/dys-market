Privacy

What we collect

  • Visits. We count page views with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies or follow you to other sites. We see totals like which pages are popular and where visitors come from, not who you are.
  • Things saved on your device. Your collection, want list, calculator numbers, settings and theme are saved in your own browser. We don't receive them unless you sign in and save them, or share a link.
  • Store account (optional). If you sign in with your Drive Your Style store account (run by Shopify), we receive your first name, email address and account ID, and we save your binder (cards, quantities, grades and what you paid) to your account.
  • Public binders. If you make your binder public, anyone can see your display name, your cards and their quantities. Your email and what you paid are never shown. Likes and follows are public too. You can make your binder private at any time.
  • Shared links. A collection share link carries your list inside the link itself. Anyone you send it to can see that list.
  • Offers. Drive Your Style may send offers on cards in public binders. Offers and replies are kept in your private inbox.
  • Card scanner. The scanner uses your camera inside your browser. Images stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Messages. When you email us or use our store's contact form (for example to sell us cards), we use what you send to reply and make an offer.

How we use it

To run the site's features, answer you, make offers you asked for, and improve the site. We don't sell your personal information or use it for advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site: GitHub (hosting), Shopify (store accounts and our store) and GoatCounter (visit counts), each under their own privacy terms, or when the law requires it. Our store's own privacy policy is at driveyourstyle.net/policies/privacy-policy.

Links and affiliate links

The site links to other sites such as TCGplayer, eBay, StockX and GOAT. Some eBay links are affiliate links (eBay Partner Network): if you buy through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, and eBay may set its own cookies. Their privacy policies apply once you leave our site.

Your choices

  • Clear what's saved on your device by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Make your binder private, or email us to delete your binder, likes, follows and offers.
  • Manage or delete your store account through our store or by emailing us.

Children

The site isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security and keeping data

We keep account data only as long as your account or binder exists, and we protect what we store with access controls and encryption. No site is perfectly secure, so please don't put sensitive information in your binder name or alias.
