Shopify importer

Pick sealed products or singles, set quantities and tap Add to store. New products go live at today's TCGplayer market price; items already in your store get restocked. Prices then follow the market every night, with a safety net.

Store connection & price safety net

Paste your GitHub key (fine-grained, Actions: Read and write on dys-market). It stays on this device only.

  • Prices update nightly to the market price.
  • If the market drops more than 15% in one night, the price is held and listed below for you to review.
  • Prices never go below Your cost (Shopify's "Cost per item").
  • Jumps over 50% in one night are held too (usually bad data).
