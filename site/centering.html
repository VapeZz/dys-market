How to get an accurate read

  • Lay the card flat on a dark, plain surface. Light cards on a white table are hard to find.
  • Hold your phone straight above the card, not at an angle. Angled shots squeeze one side.
  • Use Straighten if the card is tilted in the photo, then drag each line onto its edge. The zoom shows you exactly where it sits.
  • Do the back too. PSA allows more on the back, but it still counts.
  • A phone photo is usually within 1 to 2 points of a flatbed scan. If you're right on a limit, scan it to be sure.
