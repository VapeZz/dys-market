Grading Insights

Find the cards worth sending to PSA, CGC, BGS or TAG. Set your real grading cost and your odds of a 10, and every card shows the graded price it needs to sell for before grading pays off.

How the estimate works

Only cards with a steady price and live listings near the market price are shown, so thin or spiking prices don't mislead the math. Promos, event and prize cards are left out. All-in cost = the lowest raw listing today + your grading cost + shipping and insurance. Break-even 10 price = the graded 10 price where the expected value of grading equals the all-in cost, using your odds: a 9 sells for the multiple of raw you set (1× by default) and anything lower for about 0.7× raw. The lower the break-even compared to raw (shown as ×), the easier grading pays off.

Graded prices are recent eBay and Fanatics sold prices for each grade (PokemonPriceTracker), currently for English Pokémon cards and growing daily. Cards are ranked by expected profit: (chance of 10 × 10 price) + (chance of 9 × 9 value) + (rest × lower value) − all-in cost. Selling fees and taxes are not included. Odds are your estimate, not a prediction; check centering and surfaces first. Estimates only, not financial advice.
